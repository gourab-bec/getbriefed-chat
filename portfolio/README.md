Agents do 100% of: research, drafting, planning, content, packaging, tracking, reporting,
and escalation triage. Agents can NEVER do: appear on camera, sign, pay, send from your
personal accounts, accept legal/fiduciary duties, or attend your county/NABC meetings.
Those land in ONE consolidated founder queue. **Total founder budget: ~7 hrs/wk** —
MHLive 4h (2h recording + 2h review/sends) · ZipNab 2h (weekly review + escalations) ·
Data&AI 1h (approve proposals/outreach) — leaving your job and civic work untouched.
