# CLINKIT STORE PARTNER TERMS (Optional Deals Program)

> **DRAFT — ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED.** For local stores that opt in to publish deals or
> connect POS inventory (Briskly/Square). Participation is free and optional — Clinkit
> Runners shop retail at any store regardless of participation.

**1. Program.** Store may (a) claim its Clinkit profile, (b) publish deals/promotions, and
(c) connect a POS/inventory feed so Clinkit displays live prices and stock with
`confidence: 1.0` ("Verified Partner" badge).

**2. No purchase agency; no resale by Clinkit.** Runners purchase at Store's posted retail
prices as ordinary retail customers on behalf of buyers. Clinkit takes no title to goods,
charges Store nothing, and owes Store nothing except accurate display of Store-provided
data. Store's own terms of sale, returns, and receipts govern the retail transaction.

**3. Store data license.** Store grants Clinkit a non-exclusive, revocable license to
display Store's name, location, logo, prices, stock, and deals in the Clinkit apps. Store
warrants its feed is accurate and lawful (e.g., no MAP violations, no restricted-item
promotions). Clinkit displays freshness timestamps and is not liable for Store feed errors,
but will correct or remove data on notice within 24 hours.

**4. Deals.** Store controls deal content, timing, and inventory limits; deals are honored
at the register by Store (Clinkit does not fund them unless separately agreed in writing).
Deals must comply with pricing/advertising law.

**5. Data back to Store.** Clinkit may share aggregated, de-identified demand data
(top-searched items, fulfillment rates) with Store. No buyer personal information is shared.

**6. Term.** Either party may terminate at any time with notice; on termination Clinkit
removes the badge, feed, and deals within 48 hours (cached prices may persist up to the
stated cache TTL).

**7. Liability.** Each party bears its own; neither is liable to the other for indirect
damages; Clinkit's aggregate liability under these terms is capped at $500 (the program is
free). California law; San Joaquin County venue; individual arbitration mirror of Buyer ToS §9.
