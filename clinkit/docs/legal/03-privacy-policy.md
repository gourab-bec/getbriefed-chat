# CLINKIT PRIVACY POLICY

> **DRAFT — ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED.** GetBriefed Inc/LLC d/b/a ZipNab. Effective {{Date}}.

**1. What we collect.** Account data (name, email, phone, password hash); addresses and
geolocation (buyer drop-off; Runner GPS while online/delivering only); order contents;
payment tokens (Stripe holds card data — we never see card numbers); photos Runners take as
proof (receipt, delivered goods); device and log data; communications (in-app chat).

**2. How we use it.** To run the marketplace: price comparison near you, matching, delivery
tracking, payment, fraud prevention (e.g., receipt-total vs charge checks, geofence
verification), support, legal compliance (tax, 1099), and product improvement with
aggregated/de-identified data.

**3. Sharing.** With the Runner/buyer on your order (first name, address as needed,
photos); processors (Stripe, Avalara, AWS, Checkr for Runner checks, Twilio); store/price
data providers receive queries, never your identity; authorities when legally required.
**We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.**

**4. Retention.** Order records: as required for tax/audit (7 years, financial fields
only). Runner GPS trails: deleted 30 days post-delivery. Chat: 90 days. Proof photos: 180
days or until dispute closes. Deleted accounts: purged within 30 days except legal holds.

**5. Your rights (CCPA/CPRA — applied to all users regardless of state).**
Know/access/export (in-app: Profile → Export my data, or `GET /api/auth/me/export`);
delete (Profile → Delete account); correct; limit use of sensitive PI (precise location is
used only to provide the service — a permitted purpose); non-discrimination. We honor
Global Privacy Control signals on the web app. Requests: privacy@getbriefed.example;
verified within 45 days. Authorized agents accepted per CCPA.

**6. EU/UK visitors (GDPR).** Legal bases: contract (service), legitimate interest (fraud,
security), consent (marketing). Rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability,
restriction, objection; complaints to your supervisory authority. We do not target the
EU/UK at launch; data is processed in the United States.

**7. Security.** TLS in transit, encryption at rest (RDS/S3), scrypt password hashing,
least-privilege access, secrets in AWS Secrets Manager, audit logging of admin actions.
Breach notification per applicable law without undue delay.

**8. Children.** Not directed to under-16s; we do not knowingly collect their data.

**9. Changes.** Material changes announced in-app 14 days ahead; archive of prior versions
available on request.

**Contact.** GetBriefed Inc/LLC d/b/a ZipNab — privacy@getbriefed.example (activate real
inbox before launch; also update §11 of the Buyer ToS).
