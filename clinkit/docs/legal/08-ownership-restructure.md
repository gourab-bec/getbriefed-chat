# Ownership Restructure — 51% Sompriya Chanda / 49% Co-Founder

> **DRAFT — ATTORNEY + CPA REVIEW REQUIRED.** Transferring membership interest has tax
> consequences (gift vs. sale, basis, community-property elections for spouses in CA).
> Between spouses this is usually simple and tax-free, but get 30 minutes of CPA time
> before signing. Nothing here takes effect until executed and filed.

## Why 51/49
Women-owned certifications (SBA **WOSB**, CA **WBE/DGS**, WBENC) require ≥51% ownership
**and control** by a woman: Sompriya Chanda must hold majority interest AND serve as the
highest officer (Managing Member/CEO), managing day-to-day operations. Paper ownership
without control fails certification audits — the operating agreement below therefore
vests management authority, not just units, in her.

## Step-by-step (maps to dashboard Task 3)
1. Confirm current entity type + state (Q&A item `ENTITY_STATE`). If **LLC**: amend the
   Operating Agreement (template below) + file any state amendment listing managers.
   If **Inc (corporation)**: instead execute a stock transfer + update the stock ledger,
   and consider LLC conversion for pass-through simplicity — ask the CPA.
2. Execute the **Membership Interest Assignment** (co-founder → Sompriya Chanda, to reach
   51%). Spousal transfer: typically gift-tax-free (unlimited marital deduction); CPA
   confirms and papers basis.
3. Adopt **Amended & Restated Operating Agreement** naming Sompriya Chanda as sole
   Managing Member with operational control; co-founder as Member (49%) with defined
   consent rights (sale of company, new debt > $25k, admission of members).
4. File/update: IRS Form 8822-B if responsible party changes · CA SOS Statement of
   Information (manager change) · d/b/a "ZipNab" filing lists the LLC · bank signature
   cards · Stripe beneficial-ownership update (>25% owner — Stripe will ask).
5. Certifications (post-restructure, ~90 days): SBA WOSB via certify.sba.gov (free) ·
   CA DGS WBE · optional WBENC (fee). Needed docs: amended OA, assignment, tax returns,
   resume showing her control.
6. Business license: the **City of Tracy business license** and CDTFA permit are issued to
   the LLC — file them AFTER the amendment so records show the final structure.
