## TikTok / Reels scripts (15–30 s, founder-voiced, phone-shot)

**1. "The receipt test" (hero ad)**
> [Hook, 2s: two receipts side by side] "Same milk. Same day. $2.99 here, $3.89 there —
> SAME TOWN." [Screen-record ZipNab compare: WinCo card with 🏆 CHEAPEST badge] "ZipNab
> checks every store within 10 miles and a neighbor delivers the cheap one in under an
> hour." [CTA] "First order? Code TRACY7 takes $7 off. Link in bio."

**2. "Runner POV"**
> [Dash-cam/cart POV] "I made $214 Saturday just buying groceries for neighbors. I bid my
> own fee, no boss, no schedule. ZipNab pays the moment I deliver." [Show payout screen
> $11.25] CTA: "Runners get $30 on their first delivery this month."

**3. "The lazy tax"**
> "You're not paying for delivery. You're paying for NOT knowing eggs are 80 cents cheaper
> across the street. We know. We check. We bring them." [Compare screen scroll]

**4. "Surge honesty" (trust-builder)**
> "Busy Friday? We show you the surge number BEFORE you order. No hidden juice. The shelf
> price never changes — ever." [Point at provenance chip: 'price ✓ 12m ago']

## Landing page copy (drop into web/app/page.jsx hero when launching)
- H1: **Every store's price. One hour to your door.**
- Sub: ZipNab compares live prices at {STORE_A}, {STORE_B}, Walmart, Target and every
  store within 10 miles — then a neighbor delivers the cheapest basket. You see the shelf
  price, the runner's fee, and the delivery cost before you tap. No warehouse. No markup
  games. No membership.
- Bullets: 🏆 Cheapest-store badge, backed by timestamps · 🏃 Neighbors, not vans — they
  bid for your order · 💸 First order $7 off — code {CITY}7
- Runner section H2: **Set your own fee. Keep your own hours.** "Average active runner in
  {CITY} earns $18–26/hr engaged time including tips. $30 first-delivery bonus this month."
  (⚠️ substantiate earnings claims with real corridor data before scaling paid spend — FTC.)

## Rules
No competitor disparagement by name in paid ads; price claims must be reproducible in-app
same-day (screenshots auto-dated); "cheapest" always tied to "at time shown"; referral and
earnings claims carry required FTC disclosure lines; store logos only in organic
screenshot context (nominative fair use), never in composed paid creative.
