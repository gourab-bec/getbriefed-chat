# ZipNab Content Studio (Content-Agent Playbook)

Produces every asset for the plan in 03. Video strategy: **photoreal gen-AI for product/
lifestyle b-roll + real humans for anything testimonial-shaped.** AI-fabricated "customers"
or "runners" giving experiences = illegal under the FTC fake-testimonial rule; it's also
lower-converting than authentic UGC. Disclose AI where a reasonable viewer would care.

## 1. Hero videos — generation-ready specs (Sora / Veo / Runway / Firefly)

**H1 · "The Receipt Test" (15 s, the money ad)**
- Shot 1 (2 s): *"Macro shot, two crumpled grocery receipts side by side on a kitchen
  counter, morning light, identical items visible, totals $47.83 and $39.12 circled in
  green marker, photorealistic, shallow depth of field, handheld feel."*
- Shot 2 (5 s): screen-record the REAL app (no gen-AI): compare card, 🏆 CHEAPEST at WinCo,
  "price ✓ 12m ago" chip. Real UI builds trust and is FTC-safe.
- Shot 3 (5 s): *"Photorealistic suburban Central-Valley doorstep, golden hour, a paper
  grocery bag handed from a smiling delivery runner to a resident, candid phone-video
  aesthetic, slight camera shake, no logos."*
- Shot 4 (3 s): logo card + "Nab it cheaper. Get it faster." + code {CITY}7.
- Voiceover (record real, founder or $50 VO): "Same groceries. Same town. Thirty-one
  dollars different. ZipNab checks every store near you — a neighbor delivers the cheapest
  one in under an hour."

**H2 · "One Hour, Every Store" (20 s, brand)** — drone-style gen prompt: *"Aerial dusk
shot slowly descending over a small California suburb, warm streetlights, five different
grocery-store parking lots visible in one frame, cinematic, anamorphic"* → overlay animated
price pins ($2.99 · $3.24 · $3.49 · $3.89) converging on one green 🏆 pin → cut to real app
→ end card.

**H3 · "Runner Freedom" (30 s, supply-side)** — REAL runner (first 10 recruits; $100 +
consent release for footage), day-in-life vertical: accept → shop → deliver → payout screen
$11.25 + tip. Gen-AI only for b-roll (steering wheel, sunrise parking lot). Overlay:
"You bid your fee. Prop 22 floor guaranteed. $30 first-delivery bonus." Disclosure line:
"Paid partner. Earnings vary; see zipnab.com/runner-pay."

**Cut matrix per hero:** 9:16 (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) · 1:1 (feed) · 16:9 (YouTube) ·
first-frame hook text ≤ 8 words · captions always on · export 1080p, <15 MB.

## 2. Always-on organic (1/day/city, 10-min production)
Mon receipt-test remake w/ this week's real prices · Tue "cheapest store this week" data
post (auto-generate from quote engine — our proprietary content moat) · Wed runner story
(real) · Thu price-myth ("delivery apps mark up 15%… we can't — the shelf price is on your
screen") · Fri weekend-basket deal · Sat community reply/duets · Sun founder build-log.

## 3. Static/flyer kit
Firefly/DALL·E prompts from 02 §image-prompts + QR + {CITY}7. Apartment lobbies, laundromats,
campus boards, church halls (ask first — the ask itself is marketing).

## 4. Landing pages
Waitlist page (pre-launch): H1 "See what milk really costs in {CITY}." Email+ZIP only,
live compare-widget demo with mock data, share-to-jump-queue. Post-launch: hero copy in
02 §landing. A/B: savings-led vs speed-led H1 (review agent decides at n≥1,000 visits).

## 5. Paid campaign structures (launch-ready; YOU authorize spend per tier)
- **Meta** — CBO campaign per city: ad set 1 deal-hunters (interests: coupons, Aldi, WinCo,
  grocery budgeting) · 2 broad 25–54 geo 8-mi · 3 retarget site/social 30d · 4 runner
  recruit (gig-work interests, separate campaign, "Special Ad Category: employment" NOT
  required for IC gigs — verify at setup). Start $40/day/city, H1+H2+top organic.
- **TikTok** — Spark Ads on the two best organics, $25/day/city, broad + interest stack.
- **Google Search** — exact/phrase: "grocery delivery {city}", "cheapest grocery delivery",
  "instacart alternative cheaper", "{store} delivery {city}". $15/day/city, ROAS gate 2.0.
- **Kill/scale (review agent enforces):** kill creative at CAC > $12 after $150; 3× budget
  on CAC < $6 winners; runner campaigns gate at < $40/activated runner.

## 6. Compliance rails (every asset, no exceptions)
Real prices with date proof · earnings claims only with corridor data + "earnings vary"
link · incentivized posts disclosed (#ad) · no competitor names in paid · no AI
"testimonials," ever · store logos only in genuine app screenshots (nominative use).
