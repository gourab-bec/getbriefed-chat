# ZipNab Marketing Plan (Planning-Agent Output, v1 — 2026-07-31)

Product truth the whole plan sells: **ZipNab shows every store's real price and a neighbor
delivers the cheapest basket in under an hour. No warehouse. No markup games.**
Enemy: the hidden markup (Instacart marks up shelf prices ~10–15%; we show the shelf price
with a timestamp). We will not out-spend incumbents; we out-*trust* them, corridor by corridor.

## 1. Honest targets (what the money math actually supports)

Contribution today ≈ $0.51/order ($35 AOV). Levers that raise it (sequenced below):
bigger baskets (+$0.66), $1.49 service fee on sub-$25 orders, ZipNab+ subscription
($4.99/mo, month 4+), store-funded deals (month 6+). Blended contribution reaches
**~$2.20/order by month 9** on the lever schedule.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 corridor | 12 | −$1.4k (planned burn, inside $5k) | CAC < $12 |
| 2 | 1 | 30 | **−$0.6k** ← the honest month-2 number | retention ≥ 30% |

| 12 | 8 (needs seed ~$1.5M) | 1,700 | +$35k | referral share ≥ 30% |
| 18–24 | 15–20 | ~15,000 | **$100k+/mo net** | the target, reached honestly |

**"Thousands of sign-ups at launch"** is achievable as *sign-ups* (waitlist), not orders:
the pre-launch waitlist play below targets 2,000–5,000 corridor sign-ups in 3 weeks at
~$0.40–0.80 each. Orders then convert from that list at 8–15%.

## 2. Audiences (per city, cloned)
- **Deal-hunter households** (25–54, suburban, grocery-price-sensitive; Facebook groups,
  Nextdoor, coupon TikTok). Message: the receipt test. First-order code {CITY}7.
- **Busy families / no-car / mobility-limited** (convenience + savings). Message: "your
  town's lowest price, delivered by a neighbor."
- **Runners** (gig workers multi-apping, students, retirees; FB gig groups, r/couriersofreddit,
  campus boards). Message: bid your own fee + Prop 22 floor + $30 first-delivery bonus.
  **Supply always 2 weeks ahead of demand** — runner campaigns start Week −2 in every city.

## 3. Channel plan by budget tier (activate the tier you've funded — never ahead)

**Tier A — $2.8k (now, corridor):** organic TikTok/Reels daily (content studio 04),
Nextdoor founder posts, FB groups, 1,000 flyers, $1.7k boosted geo-fenced spend on the two
best organics, referral engine ($7/$7, $30 runner). Goal: 30 orders/day, CAC ≤ $8.
**Tier B — +$30k (post-Chase/SBA):** Meta + TikTok paid (structures in 04 §5), Google
Search on "grocery delivery {city}" + "instacart cheaper alternative", YouTube Shorts,
Spanish-language creative (40% of Central Valley), 2 new cities. Goal: 260 orders/day.
**Tier C — +$250k+ (seed):** streaming audio/CTV local, micro-influencer program
(20 × $250–1k/city, whitelisted ads), PR ("the app that shows the price Instacart hides"),
campus ambassadors, retail-media partnerships. 8+ cities.

## 4. The flywheel + pre-launch waitlist play
Waitlist page ("See what milk really costs in {CITY} — first 500 get $10 credit") runs 3
weeks pre-launch per city; every sign-up asked to share for queue-jump (viral loop k≈0.3).
Density → runner earnings → faster acceptance → conversion → savings screenshots shared →
CAC falls. Kill/scale rules and all creative live in 04; daily enforcement by the review
agent (05).

## 5. What we never do
Buy fake reviews/testimonials (FTC ban — real users only, disclosed incentives) · run
earnings claims without corridor data · disparage competitors by name in paid ads ·
launch demand before supply · spend ahead of the funded tier.
