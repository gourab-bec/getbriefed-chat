# Hyper-Local Launch Playbook — Tracy / Mountain House / Stockton (clone per city)

Budget: **$2,800 media + $600 runner bonuses + $420 buyer credits** (fits Phase-1 budget).
Core message: **"Cheaper than Amazon, faster than anyone — one-hour local steals from
WinCo, Walmart, Target, and every store within 10 miles."**
Proof device in every asset: a real side-by-side price card screenshot (milk $2.99 WinCo vs
$3.89 Safeway) — the product IS the ad.

## Week-by-week (first 30 days)

**Week −1 (supply first — runners before buyers):**
- Recruit 15–20 runners: Facebook gig groups (Tracy/Stockton Jobs, Side Hustle CA Central
  Valley), Craigslist gigs ($0), Nextdoor personal post, campus boards at Delta College &
  UOP Stockton. Offer: "$30 bonus on your first completed delivery + you set your own fee
  (10% default). No schedules. Keep 100% of tips."
- Gate: ≥10 activated runners (checklist in docs/ops/01) before any buyer ad runs.

**Week 1 (density bomb, one ZIP only — 95376 first, not 95391):** start where stores are
inside the free-delivery zone so the cheapest-badge story is strongest.
- TikTok/Reels organic: 1/day from the creative kit (02), founder-voiced.
- $500 boosted spend on the 2 best organics, geo-fenced 5-mi radius, broad 21–55.
- Nextdoor: founder "neighbor" post (not ad) + business page. Local FB groups (Tracy Buy
  Sell, Mountain House Happenings): value post with the price-comparison screenshot.
- 1,000 door flyers ($180 print) at apartment complexes near WinCo/Walmart corridor — QR →
  $7 first-order credit (code TRACY7).
- Reddit r/Stockton + r/CentralValley: authentic founder AMA-style post; no astroturf.

**Weeks 2–4:**
- $1,200 paid: duplicate winning creative into Mountain House + Stockton ZIPs; retarget
  site visitors ($3–5 CPM local).
- Referral engine (live in-app: `/api/referrals`): $7/$7 buyer↔buyer, $30 runner. Push
  share-code card after every completed delivery ("Your neighbor saves $7, you get $7").
- Store-partner outreach: 10 local independents get the free Deals program pitch
  (04-store-terms.md) — each signed store is a co-marketing channel to its own customers.
- Weekly public "price report": "This week milk was cheapest at ___" — organic SEO/social
  asset generated from our own quote data.

**Kill/scale rules:** creative with CAC > $12 after $150 spend dies; winners get 3× budget.
Any ZIP reaching 8 orders/day unlocks the next adjacent ZIP (density before breadth).

## The flywheel (how this compounds to the Phase-1 targets)

order density ↑ → runners/hour ↑ → acceptance < 90 s & delivery < 40 min → conversion ↑ &
surge ↓ → effective delivery fee ↓ (route stacking, P1 feature) → price-led word of mouth
(savings screenshot is inherently shareable) → CAC ↓ as referral share of signups ↑
(target: 40% by month 3). City clone = rerun this file with new store fixtures + ZIPs;
every asset below is templated on {CITY}, {STORE_A/B}, {PRICE_A/B}.

## KPI targets (weekly review vs `/api/admin/metrics` + docs/ops/04)
CAC ≤ $8 blended · first-order → second-order ≥ 45% within 14 days · 4-week retention ≥
35% · runner utilization ≥ 1.5 deliveries/online-hour · referral share ≥ 25% by week 6 ·
NPS ≥ 60 on post-delivery 1-tap survey.
