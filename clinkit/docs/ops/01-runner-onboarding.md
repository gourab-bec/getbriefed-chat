# Runner Onboarding Checklist + Training Script

## Activation checklist (all gates enforced before first order; see legal/06)
1. [ ] App signup as Runner → Stripe Connect Express onboarding link (identity + bank; Stripe handles KYC + future 1099s)
2. [ ] Upload: driver's license, vehicle registration, auto-insurance declarations page (expiry captured)
3. [ ] Acknowledge personal-auto commercial-use exclusion warning (verbatim screen, logged)
4. [ ] Sign Runner ICA + FCRA background-check consent (DocuSign envelope, templates in docs/legal)
5. [ ] Background + MVR check clear (Checkr)
6. [ ] Watch 4 training videos below (in-app, ~6 min total) + pass 5-question quiz
7. [ ] First delivery: flagged "new runner" — ops watches proof photos live; $30 bonus releases on completion

## Training video scripts (30–90 s each, founder-shot, vertical)

**V1 — How you get paid (60s).** "Three numbers on every request card: item reimbursement —
you never spend your own margin; your fee — you bid it, 5 to 20 percent, default 10; the
delivery fee. Clinkit takes 5% of the job total, you keep the rest plus 100% of tips. Money
lands via Stripe after the buyer's payment captures at delivery. Surge multiplies YOUR fee,
never the shelf price — the app shows it before you accept. And in California you have a
floor under all of it: Prop 22 guarantees at least 120% of minimum wage for your engaged
time plus 37 cents per engaged mile, checked every two weeks — if your earnings ever land
under that line, we pay the difference automatically. Watch it live in Earnings → Prop 22."

**V2 — The shop (90s).** "Accept → navigate to the store shown, not your favorite one —
the buyer picked it for its prices. Work the checklist: Found, Substitute, or Out. NEVER
substitute without the buyer tapping yes in chat. At checkout: photograph the itemized
receipt BEFORE leaving the register — no receipt photo, no payment release. If the total
runs more than $2 over the estimate, the app pauses for buyer approval — wait for the green
check."

**V3 — The delivery (45s).** "Follow the pin, hand it over or use the safe-drop spot from
notes. Photograph the goods AT the door — that photo is your protection against
false-non-delivery claims. Tap Delivered. That's the moment payment captures and your
payout is locked."

**V4 — Staying independent (30s).** "You choose when to log on, which jobs to take, what
to bid. You can run other apps at the same time. Nobody at Clinkit schedules you or can
require you to accept anything. That independence is real and it's the deal."
