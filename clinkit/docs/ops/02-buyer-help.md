# Buyer Help Center (seed articles)

### How does ZipNab find the cheapest store?
We pull prices from store APIs and licensed data sources for every store within your
radius, then rank by your **true total** — shelf prices + your Runner's fee + delivery +
tax — not the shelf price alone. Every price shows its source and how fresh it is ("✓ 12m
ago"). Sometimes a slightly pricier store wins because it's much closer: less delivery
distance, lower total. The 🏆 badge always means "lowest total for your whole basket."

### Why did my total change after shopping?
Estimates become actuals when your Runner scans the receipt. Small drifts (± a few cents
per item) apply automatically; if the total runs **more than $2 over the estimate**, we
pause and ask you to approve before the Runner pays. Out-of-stock items are removed. Your
receipt in the app always matches the store receipt photo.

### What's the Runner fee and surge?
Runners are independent neighbors who bid a procurement fee (5–20% of items, usually 10%).
At busy times a surge multiplier (shown before you order, e.g., "⚡1.3×") applies to the
Runner fee only — never to shelf prices, never hidden.

### Substitutions
You pre-approve substitution preferences per item, and your Runner must get your in-chat
yes for anything else. "Refund the item" is always an option.

### Something's wrong with my order
Report within 48 hours from the order screen (missing/damaged/wrong item, late, not
delivered). We check receipt + delivery photos and GPS and resolve within 24h: redelivery,
credit, or refund. Perishable issues: add a photo, we don't quibble on obvious spoilage.

### Payments & tips
Card is authorized when you accept a bid and charged only at delivery. 100% of tips go to
your Runner. Referral credits apply automatically to your next order (Profile → Referrals
for your $7 share code).

### Your data
Export or delete everything: Profile → Privacy. GPS trails auto-delete after 30 days,
chat after 90. We never sell your data. Full policy: zipnab.com/privacy.
