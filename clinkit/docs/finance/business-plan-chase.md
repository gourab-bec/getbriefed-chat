# ZipNab Business Plan — Bank Funding Package

**Prepared for:** Chase Business Banking / SBA lending conversation ·
**Entity:** GetBriefed LLC d/b/a ZipNab (51% Sompriya Chanda — women-owned; 49% co-founder) ·
**Ask:** $50,000 SBA-guaranteed facility (structure options in §7) · **Date:** July 2026

## 1. Executive summary
ZipNab is a hyperlocal delivery marketplace: buyers see live prices at every grocery/
general-merchandise store within 10 miles, and independent-contractor Runners purchase and
deliver the cheapest basket in under an hour. Revenue = 5% platform fee on every order. No
inventory, no warehouses, no employees at launch, no receivables — a working-capital-light
model. The software is complete (34 automated tests, Prop 22 compliance engine built),
soft launch is underway in the Tracy/Mountain House corridor with $5,000 of founder
capital; this facility funds the proven-playbook expansion, not product development.

## 2. Problem & solution
Grocery prices for identical items vary 15–30% between stores in the same town, but no
delivery service exposes it — incumbents (Instacart, DoorDash) mark up shelf prices and
hide comparisons. ZipNab shows the shelf price, the source, the timestamp, and ranks
stores by true total cost. The buyer saves money on the basket, which self-funds the
delivery fee — a structurally different value proposition than "convenience at a premium."

## 3. Market
San Joaquin County: ~800k residents, ~$2.4B annual grocery spend. Serviceable corridor
launch market (Tracy/Mountain House/Stockton): ~450k residents. US online grocery: >$100B
and growing double digits. Wedge: Central Valley suburbs where Instacart coverage is thin
and price sensitivity is high.

## 4. Business model & unit economics (measured, not projected)
Per average order ($35 basket): buyer pays ~$43.24; platform fee (5%) = **$2.16 revenue**;
after Stripe processing ≈ **$0.51 contribution per order**, rising to ~$1.17 at a $60
basket. Runner earns markup + delivery fee (~$7.60) and is guaranteed California's Prop 22
floor by our built-in compliance engine — a de-risking most small competitors lack.
Take-rate levers already shipped: minimum basket, small-order fee, surge share.

## 7. Funding structure options (discuss with banker)
1. **SBA 7(a) Small Loan / SBA Express** up to $50k — likeliest fit; women-owned business
   may also qualify for SBA Community Advantage-successor programs and Chase's
   women-owned-business initiatives. Expect personal guarantee.
2. Chase Business Line of Credit — typically wants 2+ years in business; position as
   follow-on after 12 months of statements.
3. Chase Ink Business credit card ($0 to open) — bridge for marketing spend day one.
Realism note: a pre-revenue LLC will be underwritten on the guarantors; keep the ask at
$20–50k, SBA-backed, and bring the live traction dashboard to the meeting.
