# Agent 02 — Business Outreach + Research (make them crave the seat)

## Target list (build week 0; ~80 businesses in/serving MH)
Tiers: T1 beloved consumer spots (restaurants, boba, salons, gyms, tutoring, dance/karate
studios) · T2 services (realtors†, loan officers†, dentists, pediatricians, insurance) ·
T3 home services (landscaping, HVAC, cleaning) · T4 institutions (schools/PTA, CSD, youth
sports leagues, faith orgs — free, credibility). †Realtors will pay first — note for the
sponsorship phase.

## Research agent (runs per target BEFORE any message; prompt)
> Research {business} in/near Mountain House, CA 95391. Compile: owner name(s) and story if
> public; years operating; Google/Yelp rating + the 3 most-praised things and 1 recurring
> complaint; menu/service highlights; social handles + follower counts + last-post date;
> any news/community mentions; what a neighbor would most want to ask them; 3 personalized
> hooks for an interview invite ("your lamb chops have 47 five-star mentions — the town
> wants the story"). Output: one-page dossier + 10 interview questions ranked by audience
> interest. Sources cited; nothing invented — mark unknowns as unknown.

## The pitch (why they say yes — lead with their upside, all free)
One-pager (send as PDF/image): **"12 minutes. 30,000 neighbors. Free."**
- Your story, professionally shot and edited, on the channel Mountain House actually watches
- You get the full video + cut-downs to use in YOUR marketing, forever, free
- We promote every episode to the whole town (paid + organic) at our cost
- Episode day = your spotlight: pinned post, story takeover, giveaway to drive foot traffic
- Only ask: share it with your customers, and tell us your real story.
Scarcity (true, not fake): "We produce 2 episodes a week. The waitlist is first-come."

## Multi-channel sequences ⚖️ compliance first
- **Email (B2B, CAN-SPAM):** fine unsolicited to business addresses; honest subject, your
  identity, working unsubscribe line.
- **SMS/WhatsApp (TCPA):** ❌ NO cold texts to mobile numbers — autodialed/bulk marketing
  texts without prior express consent risk $500–$1,500 *per text*. ✅ Text/WhatsApp only
  after they reply to email/DM/in-person and share their number, or via a business's
  published WhatsApp Business number for one personal (non-bulk) intro.
- **Instagram/Facebook DM:** allowed from your account, personally, low volume — agents
  draft, YOU send (automation of DMs violates platform ToS and gets the account banned —
  the account IS the business, never risk it).
- **In-person (highest conversion in a small town):** agent prints the one-pager; you or a
  helper walk it in T1 doors with a QR to the booking calendar.

**Sequence (per target):** D0 email w/ dossier-personalized hook + one-pager → D2 IG/FB DM
(2 lines + the hook) → D5 walk-in or call → D8 email 2 ("your slot this month: Wed 7pm or
Sun 10am") → D12 close-out ("waitlist moving — want the next open seat?"). Stop on any no.
After ep 5 publishes, switch tone to social proof: "Amar's episode hit 6,200 views in a
town of 30k — your customers are watching."
