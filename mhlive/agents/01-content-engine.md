# Agent 01 — Content Engine (calendar, formats, Grok video prompts)

## Episode formats (rotate; each yields 1 long video + 4-6 shorts)
1. **"Inside {Business}"** — 12-min owner interview: origin story, what MH doesn't know, one giveaway for viewers. The workhorse.
2. **"MH Explained"** — 5-min: the flagpole story, why the wind, CSD budget in plain English, new school boundaries. Source-of-truth builder.
3. **"New in Town"** — 3-min: what opened/closed/coming (Costco rumors → verified answers). Highest share rate.
4. **"Neighbors"** — 8-min: the teacher, the coach, the 5am bakery worker. Emotional engine; boomers share these.
5. **"60-Second Town Hall"** — you, one mic, one hot question, answered with sources.
