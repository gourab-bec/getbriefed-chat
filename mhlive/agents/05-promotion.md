# Agent 05 — Promotion ($20/day per hero video, honest math)

## What $20/day actually buys (so targets are real)
- **Meta (FB+IG) geo 6-mi radius on 95391:** ~$4–8 CPM locally → $20/day ≈ 2,500–5,000
  impressions/day. MH has ~12–14k adults: **total-town reach at frequency ~2 in 10–14 days
  — this is why "every household sees it" is genuinely buyable.**
- **YouTube in-feed promo:** ~$0.02–0.05/view locally → $20/day ≈ 400–1,000 paid views/day.
- **10k views/video:** achievable in 2–4 weeks per hero episode as ~3–6k paid (YT+Meta) +
  organic + the guest's own share (their audience is the multiplier). **10k *likes* per
  video is not a real number in a 30k town — the honest engagement target is 400–800
  reactions/episode, which locally is monster performance.** Never buy engagement.
- TikTok: skip paid initially — its geo-targeting is too coarse for one town; organic only.

## Budget ($20/day ≈ $600/mo)
$12 Meta (60% — where MH actually is: FB groups culture + IG) · $6 YouTube in-feed · $2
reserve for boosting whichever short breaks out. Per-generation routing: boomers FB+
Nextdoor(free) · millennials/parents IG Reels+FB · Gen Z TikTok+Shorts (organic) · everyone
YouTube search ("mountain house" queries).

## Campaign structure (agent maintains; you authorize spend)
Meta: 1 campaign "MHLive-Awareness" — ad set A cold geo 6mi 25-65+ (ThruPlay objective),
ad set B engagers-retarget → subscribe/site, ad set C per-episode boost using the guest's
best 15s hook. Creative = episode cuts (real beats gen-AI for locals). Kill/scale: kill any
ad >$0.08/ThruPlay after $30; 2× the winner. Frequency cap 3/wk — "absurd number of ads"
in a 30k town = ad fatigue + backlash by week 3; saturation here is cheap, so cap it and
let guests carry repetition instead.

## The redirect phase (months 3+)
When MHLive holds 1k+ fans and inbound guest flow without paid: cut to $5/day maintenance,
shift the freed budget to bayarealive.com launch zones, and put a permanent "from the maker
of MountainHouseLive" end-card bridge on every MH video.

## Weekly report (auto, Sun)
Per video: views by channel, paid vs organic split, cost/view, subs added, guest-share
reach, inbound business requests. Red flag: paid share >70% after week 2 = content problem,
not budget problem — fix the hook, not the wallet.
