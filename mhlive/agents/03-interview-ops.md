# Agent 03 — Interview Ops (prep both sides, paper the risk)

## Guest prep packet (agent generates from the 02 dossier; send T-48h)
1-pager to the guest: what the show is, the 10 questions (verbatim — no gotchas, this is a
celebration format), what to wear (solid colors), bring one prop/product, the giveaway
mechanic, arrival time + location, "we'll cut anything you regret saying — just ask,"
release form link (sign before camera rolls), share-kit promise (their cuts within 48h of
publish).

## Host card (your side; agent generates per episode)
One page: guest bio in 3 bullets · the arc (origin → craft → town connection → ask-me-
anything → giveaway CTA) · the 10 questions with one follow-up each · 2 "gold moments" to
hunt for (from dossier: the 47 five-star lamb chops story) · pronunciation notes · the
30-second cold open you record after the interview, once you know the best moment.

## Appearance release (sign BEFORE recording — no signature, no camera)
Template at `../legal/appearance-release.md` (+PDF). DocuSign fields: {{GuestName}},
{{BusinessName}}, {{Date}}, {{Signature}}. **Draft — have your attorney review once**; if
your existing signed releases (Andy Su / Berbice) have language you prefer, drop them in
`mhlive/legal/reference/` and the agent will conform this template to them. Minors: parent/
guardian release, always. Sponsored segments: disclosure rider attached.

## Recording SOP (batch: 2 episodes, one Wednesday evening)
Setup 20 min (two chairs, key light, two lavs, phone A wide + phone B tight, room tone
30s) · 25 min/interview · cold open + thumbnail stills 5 min · b-roll at the business
scheduled separately (15 min, agent books it) · files to shared drive → agent 04 takes over.
