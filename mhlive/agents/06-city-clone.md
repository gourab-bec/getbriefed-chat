## bayarealive.com (target: launch Oct 1)

30-day outcome against SFGate/ABC7/a thousand creators. The winnable October version:
**the #1 hyper-local business show in 2–3 chosen corridors** (recommend: Tri-Valley
[Dublin/Pleasanton/Livermore] + Fremont — adjacent to MH, dense with the exact
family-business audience you've proven). Format carries over 1:1; MH becomes the show-reel
that opens Bay Area doors ("we did this for Mountain House — 40% of the town watches").
Hub-and-spoke: bayarealive.com hosts corridor playlists; each corridor gets its own guest
pipeline. Regional claim comes from stacking corridors, not from a launch-day miracle.

## gourabbasulive.com (Phase 3) — and the honest $30k/mo math
Ad revenue alone won't do it: at $4–8 RPM you'd need 4–8M views/mo. The credible engine is
**sponsor-led local media** (what every successful local network runs on):
| Stream | Unit | At scale (mid-2027 realistic; Jan 2027 = stretch) |
|---|---|---|
| Corridor sponsorships (realtors, loan officers, med/dental, auto) | $750–1,500/mo per sponsor, 4–6 sponsors × 4–5 zones | $15–30k/mo |
| Featured-episode fee (once inbound demand > slots; keep 2 free eps/wk for credibility) | $500–1,500/episode × 4–8/mo | $3–8k/mo |
| YouTube/Meta ad revenue | 300k–1M views/mo | $1.5–6k/mo |
| Events (BayAreaLive Business Awards — sponsors love trophies) | 2/yr | $10–30k/yr |
**Path to $30k/mo net: ~5 zones × ~$7k gross − editor/ads/tools ≈ $28–36k gross, $22–30k
net.** Jan 2027 requires flawless execution from tomorrow; mid-2027 is the planning case.
The personal brand (gourabbasulive) rides on top: your civic work (county transportation
advisory, NABC, Bengal investment pipeline) gives the show a public-figure arc no other
local host has — that's the "every leader notices" mechanism, and it's earned, not bought.
