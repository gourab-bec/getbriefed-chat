# Agent 04 — Post-Production + Publishing

## Edit SOP (target: raw → published in 24h; tool: CapCut/Descript — transcript-based)
Agent produces per episode an **edit decision list** from the transcript: cold-open moment
(the single best 20s, placed first) · trim to 10-14 min · cut every answer's first false
start · b-roll insert points · caption style (bold, 2-line, brand green #16A34A accent) ·
music bed (YouTube Audio Library, upbeat neutral) · end card (subscribe + next guest tease
+ "want your business featured? mountainhouselive.com/beon").
First month: you or a $50-100/episode freelance editor (Upwork; agent writes the brief and
this SOP is the spec). Descript's AI removes filler words automatically — budget 90 min/ep.

## Packaging (agent drafts 3 options each; you pick in the weekly review)
Titles that work locally: name the person + the tension/curiosity — "The man behind MH's
best lamb chops," "Why Mountain House has a flagpole problem," never clickbait that
under-delivers (small town = one burn, done). Thumbnail: guest's face large, 3-4 word bold
text, brand green bar. Chapters + full transcript on mountainhouselive.com (SEO: every "X
in Mountain House" search should land on you, not Facebook).

## Schedule
Weeks 1-2: launch calendar (agent 01) — front-loaded. Weeks 3+: **2 long/wk (Thu + Sun
10am)** + 1 short/day. Scale to daily longs ONLY when the guest waitlist exceeds 3 weeks
AND editor capacity is hired — cadence you can't sustain is worse than none.

## Cross-post matrix (every long episode fans out same-day)
YouTube (full + 3 Shorts) · Facebook (native upload full — FB punishes YT links; + page
post; you personally share to your profile; **in MH groups: post as yourself, 2×/wk max,
value-first framing "I sat down with X, here's what I learned" — group-admin goodwill is a
strategic asset, never spam it**) · Instagram (3 Reels + carousel of quotes) · TikTok
(3 cuts) · Nextdoor (the boomer channel — 1 post/ep, neighborly tone) · WhatsApp: **status
+ your existing groups + a broadcast list people OPT INTO via link-in-bio** (no bulk adds
— see 02 compliance) · email digest weekly (Beehiiv free tier).
Guest share-kit within 48h: their cuts, 3 ready captions, a printable "As seen on
MountainHouseLive" window sticker PDF — the sticker turns every storefront into an ad.
